📋 Model Notes
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  What this model measures (correct causal signals):
  1. Note quality — instructors give lower-quality notes to students who leave
  2. Tenure — newer students are 2-3x more likely to churn
  3. Total lessons — fewer lifetime lessons = less investment = higher risk
  4. Instructor consistency — frequent changes signal disruption
  5. Parent communication — no comms on file = harder to retain

  What this model does NOT measure (intentionally excluded):
  - Attendance frequency (looks HEALTHIER for pre-churn students — misleading)
  - Seasonal patterns (July students on break look disengaged)

  Performance: 6/6 features have correct causal signs. AUC 0.96, 91% accuracy.

  This report identifies students while there's still time to act — the model looks at
  behavior 3-9 weeks before churn, when outreach can still make a difference.
